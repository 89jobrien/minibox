Freeze and Promotion Status

The net-new Core/Platform freeze was lifted on 2026-08-18. All gates were green and Gate 2 was above threshold. The checklist now remains a standing promotion bar rather than a freeze.

The active promotion path is develop → staging → release → main → version tag.

Gate 4 still lacks an equivalent full CI job, and Gates 5 and 6 run in the PR/merge workflows rather than one stability-gate fan-in. Promotion therefore combines CI evidence with explicit maintainer verification.
